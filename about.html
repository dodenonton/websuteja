Our Ancestral Roots

Over Three Decades of Sacred Artistry

Founded in 1988 in the historic woodcarving village of Mas, Ubud, Suteja Wood Art was born out of a profound reverence for Bali's sacred artistic heritage. In Balinese culture, wood carving is not merely a decorative pursuit—it is an act of spiritual devotion known as Yadnya, wherein the carver channels divine inspiration (Taksu) into unformed timber.

Our founder and master sculptor, I Made Suteja, was trained in the generational traditions of the Mas carving guild. Under his visionary direction, the gallery bridges centuries-old temple iconography with contemporary interior architectural elegance, bringing the warm, organic spirit of Bali into private residences, royal estates, and boutique resorts across five continents.

Every sculpture that leaves our studio is 100% hand-chiseled from single logs of seasoned timber, carrying an authenticity and depth that machine automation can never replicate.

100% Hand-Chiseled

Zero CNC or cast duplicates. Every piece is patiently hand-carved with traditional Balinese gouges and mallets.

Kiln-Dried Timber

Scientifically seasoned below 12% moisture content to prevent cracking and warping in foreign dry and cold climates.

Certified Hardwoods

SVLK-certified Perhutani Teak (Jati), aged solid Suar, and local Hibiscus (Waru) sourced legally and sustainably.

Worldwide Crated Shipping

Certified ISPM 15 fumigated wooden crating with door-to-door insured air and ocean freight to over 45 countries.
