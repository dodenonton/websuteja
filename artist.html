Master Sculptor Profile

I Made Suteja

Founder & Senior Master Carver

Preserving the Spiritual Soul of Balinese Timber

I Made Suteja was born into a family of temple carvers in Mas Village, Ubud, the legendary epicenter of Balinese fine art. From the age of twelve, he apprenticed under village elders, absorbing the sacred proportions of traditional Balinese iconography (*asta kosala kosali*) and the subtle art of coaxing dynamic emotional depth from solid hardwoods.

In 1988, he established Suteja Wood Art to ensure that authentic hand-chiseling techniques would survive the modern era of automated mass production. Over his 35-year career, Master Suteja has sculpted monumental Garuda Wisnu Kencana deities, regal Barong temple masks, and bespoke live-edge dining tables for discerning collectors, spiritual retreats, and luxury villa estates worldwide.

“The chisel does not create beauty—it simply removes the stone or timber that obscures the soul already sleeping within the tree.”

Craftsmanship Philosophy

The 4 Stages of Balinese Wood Sculpting

01

Grain Reading & Blessing

Carefully studying the raw timber growth rings and knots. Sacred Balinese offerings are given to honor the living tree spirit before chiseling begins.

02

Rough Blocking (Ngasak)

Broad steel gouges and hand mallets remove excess timber, establishing the overall balance, weight distribution, and sacred architectural geometry.

03

Intricate Detail (Ngangsir)

Fine curved micro-chisels carve intricate praba headdresses, flowing ceremonial garments, feather textures, and serene facial expressions.

04

Organic Beeswax Finishing

Graduated dry hand sanding up to 1200 grit, burnished with pure Balinese beeswax to nourish the wood grain and produce a rich, lasting amber glow.
