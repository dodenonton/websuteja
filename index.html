About Suteja Wood Art

Established in 1988 in the world-renowned carving village of Mas, Ubud, Suteja Wood Art was founded by master sculptor I Made Suteja with a singular devotion: to preserve and elevate traditional Balinese wood sculpture into timeless heirloom art.

For more than three decades, our gallery and atelier have stood as a sanctuary of authenticity. We work with select, legally harvested Indonesian hardwoods—including century-aged solid Suar, Perhutani Teak, and Waru—each piece kiln-dried scientifically below 12% moisture content to endure generations without splitting in foreign climates.

From monumental mythological deities to bespoke live-edge dining tables and architectural 3D wall reliefs, every piece is sculpted completely by hand, carrying the sacred spirit and Taksu of Balinese artistry.

Meet The Master Sculptor

I MADE SUTEJA

I Made Suteja is a renowned Balinese wood sculptor born and raised in Mas, Ubud—the historic cradle of Bali's woodcarving traditions. With more than 35 years of hands-on mastery, he preserves sacred carving philosophies while imbuing every sculpture with his distinctive artistic soul.

In Balinese Hindu tradition, every tree possesses a living spirit. Master Suteja believes that the carver does not force form upon timber, but rather frees the sacred narrative waiting within the wood's natural grain. His work has earned international praise and is proudly displayed in private collections, sacred temples, and luxury villas across five continents.
