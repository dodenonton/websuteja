The Open Sanctuary

Where Raw Timber Awakens in Mas Ubud

Located along the sacred artisan corridor of Jalan Raya Mas, our studio is an open pavilion where visitors can touch unpolished tree logs, observe senior master carvers reading timber grain, and inhale the earthy fragrance of raw teak and natural beeswax.

Here, three generations of sculptors work side-by-side. Senior masters guide younger apprentices in the subtle sacred rhythms of Balinese ornamentation, ensuring that the UNESCO-recognized intangible cultural heritage of Ubud woodcarving remains vibrant and undiluted.
